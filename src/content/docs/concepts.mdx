## How the portal divides the chain

- [Platform](/platform/) shows which system owns each stage.
- [Golden Mission](/platform/golden-mission/) walks Observation → Verified
  Outcome across the real bindings.
- [Contract Explorer](/standards/contracts/) encodes the machine-readable
  agreements between stages.
- [Evidence](/evidence/) labels how strongly each claim holds.

Declared completion never implies verified completion — that invariant is
why the chain ends where it does.
