## The research program

ISR investigates a vendor-neutral systems contract connecting human intent
to persistent, governed, verifiable outcomes. Current public work:

- **SPEC-001 / Mission Contract** — machine-readable mission, state,
  capability, authority, budget, trajectory, evidence and verifier semantics.
- **MISSION-Bench** — fault-injected evaluation of long-horizon systems.
- **Evidence-gated completion** — `Complete ≠ Verified` as a normative
  invariant.
- **STUDY-011** — preregistered live cross-provider confirmatory study
  (470 LIVE_VALID runs); reversed two deterministic directions (C-001,
  C-007) and established C-008 (full governance stack > assurance alone).
