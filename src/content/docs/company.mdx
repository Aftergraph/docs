Infrastructure and open research for verifiable intelligent systems:
**Build. Govern. Execute. Verify.**

Aftergraph is an engineering and research organization working on the
systems layer around autonomous AI — how long-running agents execute real
work while preserving bounded authority, durable state, measurable cost,
machine-verifiable evidence, and independently verified outcomes.

> Intelligence that can act. Systems that prove why.

## What we are

- Open: research, specs, benchmarks and portals are public and reproducible.
- Evidence-first: an agent saying "done" is not the system proving the outcome.
- Compositional: existing standards are used where they solve a problem.

## What we are not (yet) — explicit non-declarations

- **Not an industry standard.** AIE is a Research Draft / experimental track.
- **ACC is not stable.** Continuity research continues; nothing is declared.
- **Brand tokens are provisional** — not trademark-cleared.
- No external submissions while the evidence audit holds them (maturity
  C+ / Provisional-D).
